Sinking funds for the things you're saving toward — a certificate, an emergency fund, a big purchase. Contribute a bit at a time and watch it grow.

Data Management & Backup

Export your budget tracking database, import existing backups, or reset your workspace. All data is saved locally on your computer.

Backup Database

Download everything — transactions, categories, budgets, accounts, bills, goals, investments and reviews — as a JSON file.

Export to CSV

Spreadsheet-friendly exports for your records or an accountant.

Restore Database

Import your previously exported JSON database. This will merge or overwrite current data.

Factory Reset

Permanently delete all data — transactions, categories, budgets, accounts, bills, goals, investments and reviews — resetting to defaults.

Client-Side Sandbox

This web application is built strictly as a client-side solution. It connects to no remote servers, meaning your financial data is fully private and resides purely in your browser's local storage. This application can be committed directly to your repository and hosted on GitHub Pages without any server-side dependencies or database configurations required!
